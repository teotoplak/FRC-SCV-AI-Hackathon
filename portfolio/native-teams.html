About Native Teams

Native Teams provides an all-in-one platform for global workforce management. Companies can onboard remote employees and freelancers in 70+ countries, handle compliant payroll, and process cross-border payments — all from a single dashboard.

The platform removes the complexity of international employment law, tax compliance, and multi-currency payments, allowing businesses to focus on building great teams regardless of geography.
