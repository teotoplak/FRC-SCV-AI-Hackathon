About Flaviar

Flaviar is a members-only spirits club that curates tasting boxes, provides exclusive access to rare bottles, and builds a community of enthusiasts who share ratings and reviews. The platform blends content, commerce, and community into a single destination for spirits lovers.

Headquartered in the US with roots in Slovenia, Flaviar grew from a small tasting-box startup to one of the largest online spirits destinations, serving hundreds of thousands of members.
