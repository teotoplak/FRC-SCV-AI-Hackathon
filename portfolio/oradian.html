About Oradian

Oradian provides a cloud-based core banking platform that helps financial institutions in emerging markets digitise operations, manage deposits and loans, and reach underserved populations. The system is designed for low-bandwidth environments and scales from microfinance institutions to mid-tier banks.

With a strong presence in West Africa and Southeast Asia, Oradian's technology enables financial inclusion by lowering the technology barrier for institutions that serve the world's unbanked and underbanked communities.
