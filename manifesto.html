Manifesto

We bet on people first.

Ideas pivot. Markets shift. Teams endure. This is what we believe — and how we invest.

"Every founder is bold by definition. You have to be — there is no map for what you're building."

Capital for the BOLD.

We partner with founders whose courage and clarity compound over decades, not quarters. We don't chase trends — we back the people building them.

Five things we believe.

1. Conviction beats consensus.

The best investments look obvious in hindsight and uncomfortable at the time. We move when we have conviction, not when the herd does.

2. Operators make better investors.

Our team has built, scaled, and sold companies. We know what month six looks like, because we've lived it. That shapes the advice we give and the timing of our cheques.

3. Early-stage is a people business.

A great team can turn a mediocre market into a category. A mediocre team can squander a great one. We underwrite founders first, thesis second.

4. Honesty is the shortest path.

We will tell you what we think, even when it's hard to hear. You deserve a partner who treats you like an adult, not a portfolio line item.

5. The work is the product.

We don't measure ourselves by decks produced or panels attended. We measure ourselves by the companies our founders build.

Bold enough?

If this sounds like the partner you want in your corner, let's talk.
